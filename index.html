Linear LDPE (LLDPE)

Granules featuring narrow molecular weight distribution, yielding superior puncture resistance and tensile strength for stretch wrapping.

Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS)

Rigid, structurally stable engineering plastic granules. Offers superior impact toughness, heat resistance, and excellent surface finishes for housing units.

Our Vision for the Circular Economy

We are driving the transition from a linear "take-make-waste" model to a circular polymer economy. By designing granules that are 100% recyclable and scaling our bio-based polymer portfolio, we help manufacturing businesses achieve net-zero product footprints.

01

Closed-Loop Processing

Establishing buy-back agreements with plastic molders to re-granulate production scrap into high-performance industrial feedstock.

02

Decarbonizing Materials

Accelerating PLA, PHA, and bio-blended granule research to completely offset petroleum inputs without sacrificing polymer physical properties.

03

Responsible Stewardship

All manufacturing nodes conform to strict environmental standards, minimizing waste-water, particulate micro-dust, and carbon footprints.
